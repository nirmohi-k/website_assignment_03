Mapping Greenhouse Gas Emissions from Agrifood Systems

This interactive map visualizes greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from agrifood systems using different projections and classification methods. Data is based on FAOSTAT, which applies Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) Tier 1 methodologies for GHG inventories.

Emissions data includes CO₂, CH₄, and N₂O contributions from agriculture, land use, and food systems. Additional information on methodology is available at: FAOSTAT GHG Methodology (PDF). Link to Colab .
